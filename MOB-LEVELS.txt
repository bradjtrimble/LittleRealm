Little Realm v42 — Mob Levels

- Individual mobs now have levels.
- Spawn points use stable levels within a species range.
- Level scales HP, attack, armor/defense, and base XP.
- Bosses get independent HP/attack/armor/XP multipliers.
- Mobs 4+ levels above the player get stacking danger bonuses.
- XP rewards fall off for lower-level mobs and become zero at 5+ levels below the player.
- Target HUD and selected world mobs display level with danger coloring.
- All tuning lives in config/game-balance.js under mobLevels and each mob's baseLevel/levelMin/levelMax.
