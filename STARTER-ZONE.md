# Little Realm starter zone (v21)

The starter region is now a 44 x 32 tile zone built around long-term RPG expansion.

- **Starter Town**: northwest safe area and player spawn. NPC placeholders are already positioned for future vendor / quest systems.
- **Farm**: southwest fenced farm with passive, targetable cows, pigs, and chickens. Their stats are editable in `config/game-balance.js`.
- **Slime Spawns**: north-central grass/dirt habitat with a small pond and passive slime population.
- **Goblin Camp**: northeast dirt clearing with palisades, tents, camp props, and concentrated aggressive goblin spawns.
- **Wilderness**: broad central/southern open space with forest clusters and roaming aggressive wolves.
- **Main Road**: two-tile-wide route connecting the major areas and continuing toward the future zone transition.
- **Next Zone Entrance**: southeast road terminus blocked by a solid barricade until the next zone exists.

The existing 1.85 camera zoom and terrain/water/collision renderers are unchanged.
