Little Realm v51 — Compact Floating Backpack + Loot UI

- Backpack is now a compact 5x4 item-only panel with a small close button.
- Loot is now a compact item list; click an item to take it.
- Backpack and loot panels can be dragged anywhere on screen by their title bars.
- Panel positions persist locally on the device/browser.
- Backpack and loot are non-modal: movement, targeting, combat, mob AI, and respawns continue while they are open.
- Backpack and loot can be open at the same time.
- Loot dragging to an open backpack slot remains supported.
- Inventory slot rearranging and drag-out disposal confirmation remain supported.
- If another mob drops loot while the loot window is already open, the new drops merge with the unclaimed loot instead of replacing it.
- Closing the loot window still abandons any unclaimed drops.
- Disposal confirmation remains modal for safety.
